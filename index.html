04 · INTERPRETATION

What Does the Visualization Tell Us?

The map and chart together provide spatial and quantitative insight into the commute dataset.

01

Origin → Destination

Each arc represents a relationship between a home location and a workplace location, converting tabular records into a visible movement network.

02

Flow Magnitude

The S000 variable represents the commute flow or job-count magnitude. Larger values are represented by wider arcs.

03

Spatial Pattern

The 3D visualization provides spatial context and helps identify the distribution and concentration of commute connections.

04

Quantitative Pattern

The Top 10 bar chart ranks the strongest recorded commute flows, making their relative magnitude easier to compare.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Spatial movement becomes easier to understand when location and flow magnitude are viewed together.

The ArcLayer explains where movement occurs, while the bar chart explains which flows are strongest. Together, they provide both spatial and quantitative insight into the commute network.
